Common Questions

Questions from Delhi business owners

How long does it take to rank on Google Maps in Delhi?

It depends heavily on your category and zone — Delhi is not one market, it's several. A business in a less-saturated Delhi locality can see movement within 4–8 weeks, the same as our satellite-city timelines. A highly competitive central-Delhi category is a different story: Arora Law Associates, in Connaught Place, took 14 weeks to reach the top 3. We give an honest, category-specific estimate during the free audit rather than a single number that applies everywhere in Delhi. Every engagement includes a 60-day progress guarantee.

Is Delhi more competitive than Gurgaon, Noida, Faridabad, or Ghaziabad?

In many categories, yes — particularly in central Delhi and established commercial areas. Delhi is the largest, most saturated market in the NCR, and some categories there are genuinely harder to break into than the same category in a satellite city. That's not a reason to avoid it; it's a reason to set realistic timeline expectations from the start, which we do during the free audit.

Does one Local SEO strategy work across all of Delhi, or do different areas need different approaches?

Different approaches. South Delhi, West Delhi, East Delhi, and North Delhi behave as distinct local search markets with different competition levels and buyer behaviour — a strategy built for South Delhi's premium clinics and salons doesn't transfer directly to a home services business in Dwarka or Rohini. We build zone-specific strategy rather than a single Delhi-wide template.

My business is on Justdial and Sulekha but invisible on Google Maps — why?

Justdial and Google Maps are entirely separate systems. Reviews, enquiries, and traffic on Justdial have zero influence on your Google Maps ranking. Google ranks businesses based on GBP optimisation, citation consistency, review velocity, and proximity — none of which Justdial activity feeds into. This is one of the most common starting points we see in Delhi, and often one of the fastest to turn around.

Which Delhi business categories do you work with?

We work with service businesses across healthcare, legal and professional services, home services, fitness, and personal services throughout Delhi. One client per category per zone — once your category is taken in your area, competitors can't access the same system.
