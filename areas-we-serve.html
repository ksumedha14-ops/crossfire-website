Why Location Matters To This Work

Google ranks the map pack by proximity, not just by category

Distance to the searcher is one of the three core inputs into Google Maps ranking, alongside how well your profile matches the search and how complete and trusted it looks. That means the strategy for a Gurgaon business isn't the strategy for a Faridabad business, even in the same category — the competitive set, the zones, and often the buyer behaviour are different in each market.

This page is the map for how we think about Delhi NCR: which markets we cover, what's genuinely different about each one, and where to go for the specifics. We're a Delhi NCR-based practice without offices in every city listed here — what we bring to each market is direct, hands-on Google Business Profile and Local SEO work for your specific location, not a franchise-style local presence.

How We Work Across Delhi NCR

Same method, market-specific execution

The underlying method doesn't change between markets: Google Business Profile optimisation, citation consistency, and review generation are the same three levers everywhere. What changes market to market is the competitive backdrop, the relevant zones within each city, and the realistic timeline — a Faridabad business and a central-Delhi business are not competing against the same intensity of competition, even in the same category.

We take one client per service category per zone in every market we work in — not just per city. Gurgaon's DLF and Sohna Road count as separate zones; Noida proper and Greater Noida count as separate markets entirely. Once your category is secured in your zone, no competitor in that area can access the same system.

If your business operates in more than one of these markets, see our guide to multi-location Local SEO — each location needs to be structured so it builds authority rather than competing with your other branches.

No. Crossfire Marketing is a founder-led, Delhi NCR-based practice, not a multi-branch agency — we don't claim a physical office in every city we serve, because we don't have one. What we provide is direct, hands-on Local SEO and Google Business Profile work for your specific location, wherever in Delhi NCR that is.

Can you help businesses outside Delhi NCR?

Right now, no — we deliberately focus on Delhi NCR rather than spreading across India, because local SEO depends on genuinely understanding a market's zones, competition, and search behaviour. That focus is a large part of why the results hold up.

How do you decide which zone my business falls into?

By your Google Business Profile's actual address and service area configuration, not just the city name — Gurgaon, Noida, and Greater Noida in particular each split into distinct local search zones with different competition levels, and we confirm the correct one during the free audit.

Do you work with businesses that have locations in more than one city?

Yes — this is a distinct part of what we do, since a business with branches in, say, Gurgaon and Noida needs each location structured and ranked independently rather than treated as one listing. See our dedicated guide to multi-location Local SEO →

Is pricing different depending on which area my business is in?
