The Basics

A citation is any mention of your name, address, and phone number

It doesn't need a link back to your website to count. Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, and dozens of smaller and industry-specific directories all hold a record of your business's NAP data — name, address, phone. Google treats agreement across these records as a trust signal, and disagreement as a red flag.

This is different from a backlink, which is a link from another site to yours and primarily affects organic (non-map) rankings. Citations primarily affect your Google Maps map pack position — which is why citation cleanup is usually one of the fastest-moving fixes in an audit, alongside Google Business Profile correction.

The most common NAP inconsistencies we find
✗
Old address after an office move. Google Business Profile gets updated; smaller directories quietly don't.
✗
Different phone numbers. A landline on one directory, a mobile number on another, an old number nobody removed.
✗
Business name variations. "Sunrise Dental" on one listing, "Sunrise Dental Clinic" on another, "Sunrise Dental & Orthodontics" on a third.
✗
Duplicate or abandoned listings. An old agency created a second profile, or a previous owner's listing was never claimed or removed.
India-Specific

Which directories actually matter for a Delhi NCR business

Beyond Google Business Profile itself — which functions as both a directory and the ranking surface — the directories that carry the most weight for an Indian local business are the ones with genuine domain authority and real user traffic: Justdial and Sulekha as the dominant India-wide directories, IndiaMART for B2B-facing categories, plus category-specific directories relevant to your industry (legal directories for law firms, medical directories for clinics, and so on).

Volume for its own sake isn't the goal — a citation on a low-quality, rarely-crawled directory does little, and in some cases a business is better off with fewer, consistent, high-authority citations than dozens of scattered, half-correct ones. We prioritise correcting the directories Google actually weighs before expanding into smaller ones.

Common Questions

Questions about citations and NAP consistency

What is a citation in local SEO?

Any online mention of your business's name, address, and phone number — on directories like Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, and industry-specific listing sites, even without a link back to your site. Google cross-references these to verify your business is real and located where you say it is. Consistency builds confidence; inconsistency erodes it.

How many citations does my business need?

There's no fixed number that guarantees ranking — consistency across what you have matters more than raw count. We typically audit and correct presence across 40+ relevant directories, prioritising the ones Google weighs most over volume for its own sake.

What happens if my NAP is inconsistent across directories?

Google's confidence in your business's legitimacy drops, which directly suppresses your Maps ranking. Common causes: an office move where only some directories were updated, a phone number change, or a business name that varies slightly between listings. Each inconsistency is a small signal that something can't be fully trusted.

How long does citation cleanup take?

The audit and correction work is typically completed within the first month, though some directories take longer than others to reflect updates once submitted. We treat this as part of the foundational month-one work, alongside GBP correction.

What's the difference between a citation and a backlink?

A backlink links from another website to yours and primarily affects organic search ranking. A citation is a mention of your NAP data — it doesn't need to link to your site, and it primarily influences Google Maps ranking. For a local business chasing map pack visibility, citation consistency usually moves the needle faster.

Do I still need citations if I already rank well on Google Maps?

Yes — citations aren't a one-time fix. Directory data drifts over time even after cleanup: a directory reintroduces an old address, a third party submits an incorrect listing, or a relocation misses a smaller directory. Check your current citation consistency — free audit →
