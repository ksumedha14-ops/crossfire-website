A Recurring Question

"We're already on Justdial and Sulekha with good reviews. Why do we still need Google?"

This is the single most common objection we hear, across every city and industry we work in. The honest answer: Justdial, Sulekha, and Google Maps are completely separate ranking systems. Your Justdial reputation, however strong, has zero influence on your Google Maps position. Google ranks purely on your GBP's completeness, category accuracy, citation consistency, and review signals on Google itself.

This is actually good news for most businesses we audit: a strong Justdial reputation means real customers who are happy to leave a review — they've just never been asked on Google specifically. Converting existing goodwill into Google-specific signals is usually one of our fastest-turnaround engagements, because the hardest part — earning customer satisfaction — is already done.

Common Questions

Questions about Google Business Profile optimization

What's the single biggest reason a Google Business Profile fails to rank?

Wrong or generic GBP category. A physiotherapy clinic listed as "Health", a salon listed as "Beauty", a law firm listed as "Professional Service" instead of "Attorney" — these generic categories match almost nothing buyers actually search. Correcting the category to the precise term buyers use is consistently one of the fastest-moving ranking fixes we make, often visible within days.

My business is on Justdial and Sulekha but invisible on Google Maps. Why?

Justdial, Sulekha, and Google Maps are entirely separate ranking systems. Reviews, enquiries, and reputation built on Justdial have zero influence on your Google Maps position — Google ranks purely on GBP completeness, citation consistency, review velocity, and proximity signals. This is one of the most common starting points we see across Delhi NCR, and usually one of the fastest to turn around.

We have multiple Google Business Profile listings for the same address. Is that a problem?

Yes — duplicate listings are one of the most damaging GBP problems a business can have. When Google detects multiple listings for the same business at the same address, it splits your reviews across them and often suppresses all of them. We identify every duplicate tied to your address, consolidate them into one verified profile, and correct all conflicting data before any ranking work begins.

How much does inconsistent NAP data actually hurt rankings?

Significantly. Google cross-references your business information across the web to verify legitimacy. When your phone number on Justdial differs from your GBP, or your address on Sulekha is an outdated location, Google's confidence in your business drops — which directly suppresses your Maps ranking. Citation cleanup across 40+ directories is consistently one of the fastest-moving ranking improvements we make, regardless of industry. See our full guide to citation building and NAP consistency →

How do you generate Google reviews without violating Google's policies?

A structured follow-up sequence timed to the moment of highest customer satisfaction, asking real customers for a genuine review. No incentivised reviews, no fake accounts, no review-gating. Google's guidelines permit asking every customer; what's prohibited is selectively asking only happy customers or offering compensation. We build systems that stay inside those rules.

How long does it take to see ranking movement after fixing GBP issues?

Most businesses across Delhi NCR see measurable movement within 4–8 weeks of the core fixes. The exact timeline depends on how competitive your category and zone are — a less-saturated market can move in weeks, while a highly competitive central-Delhi category can take longer even after the same fixes. We give honest, category-specific timelines during the free audit. Every engagement includes a 60-day progress guarantee.

Do I need a separate Google Business Profile for each branch or location?

Yes, for each genuinely separate physical location. What you don't need is a separate profile per technician or per service if you operate from one location and travel to customers — that's a service-area business configuration, not a multi-location one.
