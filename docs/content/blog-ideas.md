Mirrors the phased plan in [[../../ROADMAP.md|ROADMAP]] — this file focuses on the content angle for each idea rather than build sequencing.

## Existing blog content (published 2026-07-18)

- "Why 'Spray and Pray' Marketing Wastes Your Ad Budget" — precision targeting over broad ad spend
- "The Fastest Way to Sell Anything" — demonstration as a sales tool
- "Why Nobody Sticks Around to Read" — storytelling/retention techniques

**Gap**: none of these target local-SEO or GBP search terms directly — they're general small-business marketing advice. The ideas below are specifically chosen to close that gap by answering questions the site's own FAQ schema already poses (see the rationale in each entry).

## SEO-keyword-aligned blog ideas (highest priority — fills the content/keyword disconnect)

1. **"How Long Does It Take to Rank on Google Maps?"** — Directly answers a question already repeated, in slightly different wording, across nearly every city page's FAQ ("How long does it take to rank on Google Maps in Gurgaon/Noida/Ghaziabad?"). Strong internal-linking target: every city page FAQ can link out to this as the canonical deep-dive.
2. **"Google Business Profile Categories Explained for Home Service Businesses"** — Extends the existing home-services-hub FAQ question "What GBP category should an HVAC or plumbing business use?" into a full standalone article.
3. **"What to Do About Duplicate Google Business Profile Listings"** — Extends the Ghaziabad case study (duplicate listing suppression) already published on-site into a general how-to.
4. **"Justdial vs Google Business Profile for Local Businesses"** — Turns the recurring Justdial/Sulekha objection-handling FAQ pattern (see [[../seo/keyword-opportunities.md|keyword-opportunities]]) into standalone comparison content.
5. **"Local SEO vs Google Ads: Which Should You Choose First"** — Supports the Complete Growth System / Meta & Google Ads service tiers by giving prospects a decision framework before the sales call.
