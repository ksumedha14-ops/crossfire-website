## The free audit (lead magnet)

`audit.html` is the primary top-of-funnel offer: a free, no-obligation Local SEO audit with written findings delivered within 24 hours. Per FAQ schema (index.html), it's explicitly "completely free, no credit card, no obligation."

The audit evaluates at least these dimensions (from the "7 things we investigate" section on `audit.html`):
1. Google Business Profile status (completeness score, category selection vs. competitors, photo quality/count, service area configuration)
2. Google Maps ranking position (current position for 8–12 target keywords, local pack inclusion, geographic variation, ranking trend)
3. Website visibility (NAP consistency, local schema markup, mobile experience)
4. (Remaining 4 of the "7 things" are on-page but not fully captured in this pass — see the live page for the complete list.)
