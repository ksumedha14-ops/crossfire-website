# Ideal Client Profile

Reconstructed directly from explicit yes/no criteria published on both `index.html` ("We're specific about who we work with") and `contact.html` ("Who we work best with"). This is one of the most concretely documented parts of the business strategy — the site states its ICP in plain language rather than leaving it implicit.

## Good fit ("yes" criteria)

- Local service business — trades, clinics, home services, fitness, or professional services in Delhi NCR
- 5–50 staff (per contact.html sizing)
- Established: 2+ years operating, with existing customers and word-of-mouth — "ready to systematise online growth," not validate a new offer
- Relies on inbound leads and wants more of them from Google search, not just referrals
- Wants top-3 Google Maps visibility for near-me searches
- Ready to invest consistently in a system (₹15,000–60,000/month per contact.html) — not looking for a one-time fix
- Has tried SEO/agencies before without results and wants a founder who is personally accountable
- Willing to provide GBP access and basic business information

## Poor fit ("no" criteria) — explicitly disqualified

- E-commerce businesses or national brands with no local footprint
- Businesses in their first 6 months of operation ("validate your offer first")
- Businesses expecting meaningful results within 2–3 weeks (local SEO is framed as a 6–12 week compounding process)
- Anyone expecting guaranteed overnight rankings
- Businesses unwilling to invest in ongoing implementation (no ongoing management = disqualified, even for one-time audit/fix requests)
- Businesses primarily reliant on walk-in footfall rather than search intent
- Cheapest-option seekers — "we're not the lowest price"

## Why this matters strategically

The site states outright: "If the call makes it clear we're not a match — wrong stage, wrong budget, wrong market — we say so directly." This pre-qualification is doing real work: it appears on two separate pages (home + contact) and is reinforced by the audit funnel design ("Not sure which side you're on? The free audit will make it clear"), suggesting the audit call itself functions as the final qualification filter before a sales conversation.

## Named verticals actually served

Explicitly named across site copy and case studies: plumbing, electrical, HVAC, salons, clinics, legal. The only vertical with a dedicated landing page today is home services (HVAC/plumbing/electrical) — see [[topical-authority-plan]] for the gap between named-served verticals and built landing pages.

## Assumptions

- The ₹15K–60K/month budget band is stated on `contact.html`; whether this is a hard floor or a soft guideline for sales conversations is not stated.
- No CRM or client list is in this repo, so real client count/industry mix cannot be verified beyond what's named in on-page case studies and copy.
