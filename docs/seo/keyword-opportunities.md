## Justdial/Sulekha displacement — a distinctive recurring angle

A deliberate, repeated FAQ pattern across at least 4 pages (Gurgaon, Noida, Ghaziabad, home-services hub) targets businesses that already have visibility on Indian directory platforms but not on Google:
- "My business is on Justdial and Sulekha but invisible on Google Maps. Why?"
- "My business has strong Justdial reviews but zero Google reviews. What should I do first?"
- "We have different phone numbers listed across Justdial, Sulekha, and our website. How much does this hurt?"
- "We're already on Justdial and Sulekha with good reviews. Why do we still need Google?"

This is a market-specific wedge (Justdial/Sulekha are dominant directories in India) used to convert an existing objection ("I already have listings elsewhere") into a keyword-targeted FAQ answer. Any new geo/vertical page should consider including a variant of this question.
